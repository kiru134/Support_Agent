# Sezzle Up (Credit Reporting)

Sezzle Up is an **opt-in** program that reports payment history to credit bureaus.

- On-time installments are reported and can build credit history.
- **Late or failed payments are also reported** once enrolled — enrolling is not
  risk-free and the assistant must mention this when asked about Sezzle Up.
- Enrollment requires a linked bank account (not just a card).
- Leaving Sezzle Up stops future reporting but does not erase past reported history.

Questions about *what was already reported* to a bureau, or requests to correct a
credit report, must be escalated to a human agent.
