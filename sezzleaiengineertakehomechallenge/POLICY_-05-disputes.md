# Disputes (Item Not Received / Not as Described)

If a merchant never shipped, shipped the wrong item, or the item is materially not as
described, the shopper can open a **dispute**:

- Disputes must be opened **within 90 days** of the order date.
- The shopper must first attempt to contact the merchant and allow **15 days** for a
  response before Sezzle steps in.
- While a dispute is **under investigation, upcoming installments on that order are
  paused** — the shopper is not charged until the dispute resolves.
- Resolution typically takes 10–20 business days.

Opening, updating, or ruling on a dispute is a **human-agent action**. An assistant
may explain the process and eligibility but must escalate the actual filing.
