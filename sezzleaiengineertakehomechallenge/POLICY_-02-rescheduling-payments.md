# Rescheduling a Payment

Shoppers can move an upcoming installment's due date:

- The **first reschedule on an order is free** and can push the date **up to 2 weeks**.
- Each **subsequent reschedule on the same order costs $5** (see Fees Overview).
- A maximum of **3 reschedules per order** is allowed.
- A reschedule must be requested **at least 24 hours before** the installment due date.
  Same-day requests cannot be processed by support or the app.
- Only *upcoming* installments can be moved — a failed installment must be repaid, not
  rescheduled (see Failed Payments).

Rescheduling never changes the installment amount, only the date. Later installments
do not shift when an earlier one is rescheduled.
