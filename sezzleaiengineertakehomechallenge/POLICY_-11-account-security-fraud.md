# Account Security & Fraud

If a shopper reports an order they didn't place, a stolen card, or an account
takeover:

1. **Escalate to a human agent immediately** — fraud reports are never self-serve.
2. Advise the shopper to change their password and enable 2FA in the meantime.
3. Do not read back full payment-method details, addresses, or order contents on a
   potentially compromised account.

Time matters: flag the conversation as fraud-priority. The assistant must not make
determinations about whether fraud occurred.
