# Sezzle Virtual Card

The virtual card lets shoppers use Sezzle at merchants without a Sezzle checkout
integration.

- Issued instantly in the app; usable anywhere the card network is accepted online,
  and in-store via Apple Pay / Google Pay.
- Each virtual-card purchase creates a standard pay-in-4 order.
- The card must be **pre-loaded with an approved amount** before checkout; approval
  depends on the shopper's current limit.
- Some merchant categories (gift cards, gambling, cash-like transactions) are blocked.

Declined virtual-card transactions usually mean the requested amount exceeded the
approved limit or the category is blocked (see Supported Merchants & Limits).
