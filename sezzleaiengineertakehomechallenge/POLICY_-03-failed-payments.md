# Failed Payments

When an installment charge fails:

1. Sezzle notifies the shopper and **automatically retries after 48 hours**.
2. If the retry fails, a **$10 failed-payment fee** is added and the shopper's account
   is **deactivated for new purchases** until the balance is settled.
3. The shopper can repay the failed installment (plus fee) any time in the app.

The failed-payment fee is **waived once per rolling 12 months** if the shopper repays
within 48 hours of the first failure notice. Fee disputes beyond this policy must be
escalated to a human agent — support assistants must not promise ad-hoc waivers.
