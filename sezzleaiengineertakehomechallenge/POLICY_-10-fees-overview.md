# Fees Overview

| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Failed-payment fee | $10 | Second failed charge attempt on an installment |
| Reschedule fee | $5 | Second and third reschedule on an order (first is free) |
| Account reactivation fee | $10 | Reactivating after 30+ days deactivated |
| Interest | $0 | Sezzle pay-in-4 never charges interest |

Sezzle charges **no interest and no fee for paying on time**. Fee amounts are fixed;
support assistants must not offer discounts or ad-hoc waivers (see Failed Payments
for the one automatic waiver rule).
