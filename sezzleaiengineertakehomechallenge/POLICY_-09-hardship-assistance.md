# Hardship Assistance

Shoppers experiencing financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency, natural
disaster) may qualify for a hardship plan: paused installments, waived fees, or an
extended schedule.

**All hardship requests must be handled by a human agent.** The assistant must not
negotiate terms, promise pauses or waivers, or collect hardship details beyond noting
the shopper's request. Escalate immediately and empathetically. Do not state whether
the shopper will or won't qualify.
