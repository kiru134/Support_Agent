# Supported Merchants & Order Limits

- Shopper limits are **dynamic**: they depend on payment history with Sezzle, order
  size, and time as a customer. Limits generally start small and grow with on-time
  payments.
- Sezzle does **not disclose the exact formula or a shopper's precise limit**; the
  app shows an *estimated* spending power.
- A declined order usually means the order exceeded current spending power. Paying
  down open orders and building on-time history raises limits over time.
- Why a *specific* order was declined cannot be shared in detail — the assistant may
  explain the general factors above and must **escalate requests for specific decline
  reasons** (or demands to override a decline) to a human agent.
