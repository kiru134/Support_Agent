# Submitting your work

Thanks for taking the time — this is scoped to **~2 hours**. Stop at the budget and
write down what you'd do next; an honest "unfinished" note is graded positively.

## Ground rules
- **AI tools are expected** (Claude, Copilot, Cursor, ChatGPT — anything, including
  for scaffolding). Log your prompts in `PROMPTS.md`; how you direct AI is part of
  the evaluation, never held against you.
- All data in this package is **synthetic** — no real customer data.
- Any LLM provider works, including free local models via Ollama. Where the brief
  requires an LLM in the pipeline (AI Engineer), a solution without one does not
  meet it — if you can't access any model, email the hiring manager before starting.
- Questions? Email the hiring manager (address in the message you received). Good
  clarifying questions are a positive signal.

## What to send back
Reply to the message you received with **either** a zip of your project **or** a
private repo link (GitHub/GitLab) shared with the reviewer named in your
instructions. Check `PROMPT.md`'s Deliverables list before sending — the decision
records and iteration evidence matter more than extra features.

Please don't post the challenge or your solution publicly.

## What happens next
The team reviews against a shared rubric (design decisions, iteration, technical
depth, evaluation honesty, communication). You'll hear back within a few business
days; the follow-up interview walks through **your** decision records — be ready
to argue the alternatives you rejected.
