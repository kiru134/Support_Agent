# Payment Schedules (Pay-in-4)

Sezzle's standard plan splits a purchase into **4 equal installments over 6 weeks**:
25% at checkout, then 25% every 2 weeks. The schedule is fixed at order creation and
shown in the app under the order detail.

- The first installment is charged at the time of purchase.
- Remaining installments auto-charge to the shopper's default payment method on the
  due date (local time, morning batch).
- Shoppers can pay any installment early at no cost; early payment does not change
  later due dates.
- Some merchants offer pay-in-2 or monthly plans; the order detail is the source of
  truth for a given order's schedule.

Questions about a *specific* order's next payment date or amount must be answered
from that order's record, not from this general policy.
