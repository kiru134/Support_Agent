# Account Reactivation

A deactivated account (usually from a failed payment) is reactivated automatically
once the shopper:

1. Pays all overdue installments, and
2. Pays any outstanding fees (failed-payment and/or reactivation fee).

A **$10 account reactivation fee** applies if the account has been deactivated for
more than 30 days. Reactivation is immediate after payment; no support action needed.
Repeated deactivations (3+ in 12 months) may result in reduced order limits.
